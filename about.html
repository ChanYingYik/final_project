01 · Declaration

Tool and model

OpenAI Codex desktop agent, based on GPT-5. The agent read the project brief and supplied workbook, inspected the source subtitle files, wrote the data-cleaning and classification script, built the static site, drafted bilingual text and tested the interface.

01 · 聲明

工具與模型

使用以 GPT-5 為基礎的 OpenAI Codex 桌面代理。代理閱讀作業要求及提供的 Excel、 檢查來源字幕、撰寫清理與分類程式、建立靜態網站、草擬雙語文字並測試介面。

02 · Division of work

AI output and human responsibility

Student supplied

The final-project brief and the 519-row Excel workbook.

Agent completed

Deduplication, incomplete-row checks, category build, 50-entry source-text check, web interface and report draft.

Human judgment

Confirm that classifications fit the surrounding scene, approve the report in your own voice and explain one category orally.

Before submission

Recheck the 50 marked entries and confirm the source licence and your preferred public title.

02 · 分工

AI 產出與人的責任

學生提供

期末專題要求及五百一十九行 Excel。

代理完成

移除重複與不完整資料、建立分類、核對五十筆來源文字、製作網站介面及報告草稿。

人的判斷

確認分類是否符合完整劇情、以個人語氣確認報告，並能口頭解釋一個類別。

提交前

重新檢查五十筆標記、確認來源授權，以及你是否接受目前公開題名。

03 · Prompts

Two prompts that worked

“Read the PDF and the workbook separately. Treat the PDF as the assignment and the workbook only as data.”

“Validate every row, document duplicate and missing-value decisions, and do not invent missing sentences or translations.”

03 · 提示詞

兩個有效提示

「分別閱讀 PDF 與 Excel：PDF 是作業要求，Excel 只是資料來源。」

「逐行驗證資料，記錄重複與缺值的處理方式，不得生成缺失句子或譯文。」

04 · Agent error

The first length threshold did not fit this workbook

An early trial reused a threshold designed for aligned multi-cue windows. It placed almost all 502 pairs in one profile. Inspecting the distribution exposed the mismatch; the final rule uses 0.70 and 1.30 thresholds and shows the resulting length bands separately.

04 · 代理錯誤

第一版長度門檻不適合本 Excel

初版沿用另一組多句對齊資料的門檻，結果幾乎所有五百零二筆都落入同一剖面。 檢查分佈後發現不相容，最終改用 0.70 與 1.30 門檻，並把長度區間另行顯示。

05 · Data notes

Source, cleaning and rights

Film source
English and Traditional Chinese subtitles from Spider-Man: Brand New Day (2026).
Supplied file
Final project.xlsx, one worksheet with 519 non-empty rows in two columns.
Cleaning
Seven rows had one side missing; ten later rows repeated an earlier identical pair. The final public dataset contains 502 unique complete pairs.
Traceability
Each website record retains the original workbook row number. The report keeps full subtitle tracks out of the public folder.
Verification
The 50-entry sample uses seed 2027. It was compared with the workbook and with matching source subtitle text where the Excel preserved a complete cue.
Licence
The workbook does not state a download location or licence. Confirm the source rights before public submission.
Content
Short excerpts may include profanity, violence and mature themes.

05 · 資料說明

來源、清理與權利

電影來源
《蜘蛛俠：全新的一天》（2026）的英文及繁體中文字幕。
提供檔案
Final project.xlsx，一張工作表共五百一十九行非空資料，分為兩欄。
清理
七行欠缺原文或譯文；十行與較早的句對完全相同。最終公開五百零二組完整且唯一的句對。
可追溯性
網站每筆資料保留原 Excel 行號，公開資料夾不包含完整字幕軌。
核對
五十筆樣本使用種子 2027；在 Excel 保留完整字幕的情況下，已對照來源字幕文字。
授權
Excel 未列下載位置或授權。公開提交前必須確認來源權利。
內容
短句摘錄可能包含粗俗語言、暴力及成人主題。
