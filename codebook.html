Codebook · version 3

Four profiles with one visible decision order.

This codebook classifies Spider-Man: Brand New Day subtitles by relative length and speech-act packaging. The labels describe visible choices, not translation quality.

Decision order: calculate the normalized length ratio, assign condensation or explicitation at the outer thresholds, then compare source and target speech act inside the middle band. Congruence is the remaining category.

判定次序：先計算標準化長度比；在兩端門檻分別指定壓縮或明示； 在中間區間比較原文與譯文的言語行為；餘下句對列為對應。

English base

English words × 1.45, an approximate Han-character target.

Length ratio

Target Han characters ÷ adjusted English length.

Outer rules

Below 0.70: condensation. Above 1.30: explicitation.

Middle rule

Question/command/reaction mismatch: transposition. Otherwise: congruence.

01 · Condensation

Reduce recoverable surface material

The target keeps the interactional point of a longer English line while using fewer visible target characters.

Definition
The subtitle compresses wording or omits details that the scene, grammar or a neighbouring line can recover.
Theory source
Subtitle condensation and reduction, following Díaz Cintas and Remael.
Decision rule
Normalized length ratio is below 0.70.
Positive example
Row 3: “to tell you that sounds crazy” becomes 聽落會好癲.
Negative example
Row 6 expands “when I'm lying” into 因為你好擅長分辨 我有冇講大話.
Boundary case
Short English reactions are not automatically condensation when the target repeats their meaning in a longer complete response.

Open condensation

01 · 壓縮

減少可由情境推回的表面文字

譯文保留較長英文句的互動重點，同時使用較少可見中文字。

定義
字幕濃縮措辭，或刪去可由畫面、語法及鄰近句推回的細節。
理論來源
字幕濃縮與刪減研究，參考 Díaz Cintas 與 Remael。
判定規則
標準化長度比低於 0.70。
正向例子
第 3 行：「to tell you that sounds crazy」譯為聽落會好癲。
反向例子
第 6 行把「when I'm lying」擴展為因為你好擅長分辨 我有冇講大話。
邊界案例
短英文反應不一定屬壓縮；若譯文補成完整回應，字數可能更長。

開啟壓縮類別

02 · Explicitation

Make context or relation more visible

The subtitle uses a relatively longer target line, often completing an elliptical English clue.

Definition
The translation spells out information that is implicit in the short source cue or completes a response for subtitle coherence.
Theory source
Explicitation in translation studies, applied to subtitle rewriting.
Decision rule
Normalized length ratio is above 1.30.
Positive example
Row 6 makes the lying relationship explicit: 因為你好擅長分辨 我有冇講大話.
Negative example
Row 12 keeps “We knew each other” and 我們以前認識 in a balanced ratio.
Boundary case
A longer target may reflect cue segmentation rather than added meaning, so this label remains a surface signal.

Open explicitation

02 · 明示

讓情境或關係更清楚

譯文相對較長，常把英文省略句補成完整回應。

定義
譯文說出短原文所暗示的資訊，或補足字幕連貫所需的反應。
理論來源
翻譯研究中的明示概念，應用於字幕重寫。
判定規則
標準化長度比高於 1.30。
正向例子
第 6 行把說謊關係說得更清楚：因為你好擅長分辨 我有冇講大話。
反向例子
第 12 行「We knew each other」與我們以前認識屬均衡比例。
邊界案例
譯文較長也可能只因分句不同，因此標籤只是表面訊號。

開啟明示類別

03 · Transposition

Change the interactional frame

Within a comparable length, the subtitle changes the apparent speech act.

Definition
The pair remains in the middle length band, but question, command, exclamation or short reaction does not match across languages.
Theory source
Vinay and Darbelnet's transposition, extended here to speech-act packaging.
Decision rule
Ratio is from 0.70 to 1.30 and the source-target line functions differ.
Positive example
Row 4: “But it's true.” is a short reaction, while 但全是事實 is coded as a statement.
Negative example
Row 12 retains the statement function and is coded as congruence.
Boundary case
If a question becomes a statement outside the middle length band, the length category takes precedence.

Open transposition

03 · 轉換

改變互動框架

在長度相近的範圍內，字幕改變了明顯的言語行為。

定義
句對位於中間長度區間，但提問、指令、感嘆或短促反應在兩種語言間不一致。
理論來源
Vinay 與 Darbelnet 的詞性轉換，本文延伸至言語行為包裝。
判定規則
長度比介於 0.70 至 1.30，且原文與譯文功能不同。
正向例子
第 4 行「But it's true.」屬短促反應，而但全是事實列為陳述。
反向例子
第 12 行保留陳述功能，列為對應。
邊界案例
若提問變成陳述但超出中間長度區間，長度類別優先。

開啟轉換類別

04 · Congruence

Keep length and speech act comparable

This is the analytical baseline for balanced pairs with matching line function.

Definition
The target is comparable in relative length and speech act, with no dominant compression or explicitation signal.
Theory source
Congruence as a working baseline, informed by subtitle reformulation research.
Decision rule
Ratio is from 0.70 to 1.30 and source-target speech acts agree.
Positive example
Row 12: “We knew each other.” and 我們以前認識 keep comparable length and statement function.
Negative example
Row 6 exceeds 1.30 and is coded as explicitation.
Boundary case
Congruence does not mean literal translation; word order and lexical choices may still differ substantially.

Open congruence

04 · 對應

保持相近長度與言語行為

這是一組分析基準，用於長度均衡且功能一致的句對。

定義
譯文在相對長度與言語行為上與原文接近，沒有明顯壓縮或明示訊號。
理論來源
以對應作為分析基準，並參考字幕重寫研究。
判定規則
長度比介於 0.70 至 1.30，且原文與譯文功能一致。
正向例子
第 12 行「We knew each other.」與我們以前認識保留相近長度及陳述功能。
反向例子
第 6 行長度比高於 1.30，列為明示。
邊界案例
對應不代表直譯；詞序與詞彙選擇仍可大幅不同。

Separate field

Cantonese colloquial and written Chinese

Definition
The coding compares visible Cantonese lexical and particle features with written-Chinese forms. It does not identify pronunciation or the language actually heard.
Theory source
Register and orality in audiovisual translation, combined with Cantonese lexical description.
Decision rule
Two or more Cantonese markers produce Cantonese colloquial; one Cantonese marker with a written form produces mixed register; otherwise the cue is written Chinese.
Cantonese markers
唔, 咩, 乜, 咗, 嘅, 喺, 嗰, 佢, 睇, 傾, 畀, 搵, 冇, 係, 啱, 掂, 搞, 啲, 噉, 喇, 喎, 㗎, 囉, 咁, 攞, 掟, 老友, 死蠢.
Written markers
的, 這, 是, 不, 沒有, 什麼, 他, 她, 在, 了, 可以, 應該, 需要, 我們, 你們, 他們, 從, 與, 因為, 如果.
Boundary case
A cue may mix Cantonese rhythm with written grammar. Register is therefore a surface tendency, and detected markers appear in each entry-detail panel.

獨立欄位

粵語口語與書面中文

定義
比較字幕中的粵語詞彙與語氣助詞特徵，以及書面中文形式；不判斷實際讀音或觀眾聽到的語言。
理論來源
影視翻譯中的語域與口語性，配合粵語詞彙描述。
判定規則
兩個以上粵語標記列為粵語口語；一個粵語標記加一個書面形式列為混合語域；其餘列為書面中文。
粵語標記
唔、咩、乜、咗、嘅、喺、嗰、佢、睇、傾、畀、搵、冇、係、啱、掂、搞、啲、噉、喇、喎、㗎、囉、咁、攞、掟、老友、死蠢。
書面標記
的、這、是、不、沒有、什麼、他、她、在、了、可以、應該、需要、我們、你們、他們、從、與、因為、如果。
邊界案例
字幕可同時混有粵語節奏與書面語法，因此只列為表面傾向；每筆詞條詳情會顯示實際偵測到的標記。

Codebook log

Revision and ambiguity

Version 1: used fixed word and character bands. The 30-pair pilot overclassified longer cue fragments.

Version 2: added the normalized ratio, kept speech-act tests inside the middle band, and separated length band from translation profile. This version applies to all 502 entries.

Version 3: added Cantonese colloquial/written-Chinese register indicators and the entry-detail panel.

編碼紀錄

修訂與歧義

第一版：使用固定字詞數區間，但在三十筆前導測試中把較長字幕片段過度分類。

第二版：加入標準化長度比、把言語行為測試限於中間區間，並把長度區間與翻譯剖面分開。所有五百零二筆資料均使用此版本。

第三版：加入粵語口語與書面中文語域標記，以及詞條詳情面板。
