Learning report

What changes when spoken dialogue becomes a subtitle line?

A corpus study of 502 English-Cantonese subtitle pairs from Spider-Man: Brand New Day, focused on relative length, speech act and translation profile.

學習報告

口語對白變成一組字幕時，發生了什麼？

本研究分析《蜘蛛俠：全新的一天》五百零二組英粵字幕句對，重點是相對長度、言語行為與翻譯剖面。

01 · Origin

A side-by-side table invited a closer question

The supplied workbook presents English dialogue from Spider-Man: Brand New Day directly beside a Traditional Chinese subtitle. The shortest pairs looked almost interchangeable, while other lines seemed to add context or change the kind of utterance being made. Those visible differences led to this project: how do short subtitle pairs redistribute meaning under the constraints of reading space and conversational flow?

01 · 緣起

由並排資料表追問字幕選擇

提供的 Excel 把英文對白與繁體中文字幕並排呈現。有些短句看似可以直接對應， 另一些則補充了情境，或改變了原句的言語行為。這些可見差異促成研究問題： 字幕如何在閱讀空間與對話節奏的限制下重新分配意義？

02 · Background

Subtitling is constrained rewriting

Subtitle studies commonly describe condensation as a normal response to limited reading speed and screen space. Vinay and Darbelnet's procedures add a second perspective: meaning can be preserved while the grammatical or interactional form changes. Explicitation provides a third lens for information that becomes clearer in the target.

This project uses those ideas as a small, transparent coding system. The categories describe surface choices. They do not claim that a shorter subtitle is automatically worse or that a longer one is more accurate.

02 · 背景

字幕翻譯是有條件的重寫

字幕研究常把壓縮視為回應閱讀速度與螢幕空間的正常策略。 Vinay 與 Darbelnet 的翻譯程序提醒我們：即使語法或互動形式改變，意義仍可保留。 明示概念則有助描述譯文如何把某些資訊說得更清楚。

本研究把上述概念整理成一套簡明而可檢查的編碼系統。類別描述表面選擇， 不代表較短的字幕必然較差，也不表示較長的版本一定更準確。

03 · Gap

A flat table hides recurring patterns

A spreadsheet can preserve every pair, but it does not show whether questions and statements follow different strategies or whether expansion clusters in particular profiles. It also makes cross-filter comparison laborious. This project turns the same records into linked views so a reader can move from a broad pattern to an individual cue.

03 · 研究缺口

平面資料表不容易呈現重複模式

Excel 能保存每一組句對，卻不容易看出提問與陳述是否採用不同策略， 也不易找出擴展句集中於哪些剖面。跨欄篩選比較亦十分費時。 本專題把同一批記錄轉為互相連動的檢視，讓讀者能由整體模式追溯至單一字幕。

04 · Questions

Three questions guide the explorer

  1. Which translation profiles appear most often in the 502 pairs?
  2. How do statements, questions, commands and reactions divide across those profiles?
  3. Do longer target lines cluster with explicitation rather than congruence or transposition?

04 · 問題

三個研究問題

  1. 五百零二組句對中，最常見的翻譯剖面是什麼？
  2. 陳述、提問、指令與短促反應如何分佈於各剖面？
  3. 較長的譯句是否集中於明示，而非對應或轉換？

05 · Method

From the supplied workbook to a reviewable corpus

Source

The supplied Final project.xlsx, retaining the original workbook row for every pair.

Cleaning

Removed seven incomplete pairs and ten duplicate pairs. The final corpus contains 502 complete unique pairs.

Pilot

Tested length and speech-act rules on 30 entries, then used the revised thresholds on all pairs.

Check

A fixed sample of 50 entries with seed 2027 was reviewed against the workbook and source subtitle texts.

The normalized length ratio is the number of Han characters in the Chinese target divided by the English word count multiplied by 1.45. A ratio below 0.70 is coded as condensation, above 1.30 as explicitation, and the middle band is split by source and target speech act. A mismatch becomes transposition; the remainder becomes congruence. These are surface rules, so ambiguous cases remain visible in the data rather than being hidden by a quality claim.

Each row also records a Cantonese colloquial/written-Chinese register signal. It is calculated from a published lexical list, and every detected marker is shown in the entry-detail panel.

Representativeness limitation. The workbook was supplied as a preassembled list. The original sampling method from the full subtitle tracks was not documented, so results describe these 502 pairs rather than the complete film dialogue.

Codebook log: version 1 used fixed word-count bands, but the 30-pair pilot overclassified long Chinese cue fragments. Version 2 normalized for an approximate English-to-Han character ratio, moved speech-act transposition to the middle band, and separated length band from translation profile. After the revision, all entries received one of the four profiles and one dialogue function.

05 · 方法

由提供的 Excel 建立可檢核語料

來源

使用提供的 Final project.xlsx，每組句對保留原工作表行號。

清理

移除七組不完整句對及十組重複句對，最終保留五百零二組完整、唯一的句對。

前導測試

先以三十筆資料測試長度與言語行為規則，再把修訂門檻套用至全部資料。

核對

以種子 2027 抽取五十筆，對照 Excel 與來源字幕文字。

標準化長度比為中文譯句漢字數，除以英文字數乘以 1.45。 比率低於 0.70 列為壓縮，高於 1.30 列為明示； 中間區間再比較原文與譯文的言語行為，不一致者列為轉換，其餘列為對應。 這是一套表面規則，邊界案例仍保留在資料中，不以「品質分數」掩蓋不確定性。

每筆資料亦記錄粵語口語／書面中文語域訊號。分類依公開詞彙表計算， 所有偵測結果均可在詞條詳情面板查看。

代表性限制：原有的字幕抽樣程序沒有隨 Excel 提供。 因此分析只描述這五百零二組句對，不聲稱代表整部電影的所有對白。

編碼簿紀錄：第一版使用固定字數區間，但在三十筆前導測試中把較長的粵語片段過度列為明示。 第二版改用標準化長度比、把言語行為轉換限於中間區間，並把長度區間與翻譯剖面分開。 修訂後每筆資料都有唯一剖面及唯一下列對白功能。

06 · Analysis

Expansion is common, but congruence remains the largest profile

The Dialogue function network shows that statements are distributed across all four profiles rather than tied to one translation strategy. Questions and commands are less frequent, but their links make it possible to inspect whether a short utterance turns into a fuller target line.

The Profile × length band treemap makes the coding baseline visible: condensation occupies the compact band and explicitation the expanded band, while the balanced band contains both transposition and congruence. The Dialogue function by profile heatmap compares where reactions concentrate and whether statements remain the main population after filtering.

These views support a cautious conclusion. In this supplied list, many Chinese cues are longer than a simple character-to-word baseline, but a long cue is not automatically an explanatory translation. It may contain a complete response, a repeated name or a subtitle line that was already split differently. The profile therefore describes visible repackaging, not translation quality.

The Cantonese colloquial and written Chinese panel uses Cantonese-specific forms such as 唔, 咩, 冇 and 係 alongside written-Chinese forms such as 的, 是, 這 and 不. Most supplied cues carry written forms, while a smaller set retains Cantonese particles or colloquial wording. The label is a visible register tendency, not a phonetic identification of spoken Cantonese.

Of the 502 cues, 462 are written-Chinese dominant (92.0%), 31 are Cantonese colloquial (6.2%), and 9 are mixed (1.8%). Cantonese colloquial cues occur in every translation profile, including 10 explicitation cases, so colloquial wording is not tied to one single length strategy.

06 · 分析

擴展常見，但對應仍是最大剖面

對白功能網絡顯示陳述散佈於四種剖面，而非固定在單一翻譯策略。 提問與指令數量較少，但讀者仍可沿連線檢查短句是否變成較完整的字幕。

剖面 × 長度區間層級圖呈現編碼基礎：壓縮集中於精簡區間， 明示集中於擴展區間，而均衡區間同時包含轉換與對應。 對白功能與翻譯剖面熱圖則比較短促反應集中在哪裡， 以及篩選後陳述是否仍為主要群體。

結果支持一個較保守的結論：在提供的資料中，許多中文句比簡單字數基準更長， 但較長不等於自動增加了說明。它可能包含完整回應、重複的人名， 或只因原文與譯文的字幕分句方式不同。因此，剖面描述可見的重新包裝， 而非評價譯文優劣。

粵語口語與書面中文面板比對「唔、咩、冇、係」等粵語特徵， 以及「的、是、這、不」等書面形式。多數提供的字幕以書面形式為主， 另有一部分保留粵語語氣詞或口語措辭。此標籤是表面語域傾向，不是語音層面的粵語辨識。

五百零二筆之中，四百六十二筆以書面中文為主（92.0%），三十一筆具粵語口語傾向（6.2%）， 九筆為混合語域（1.8%）。粵語傾向句分佈於全部四種翻譯剖面，其中十筆屬明示， 顯示粵語措辭並不限於某一種長度策略。

07 · Conclusion

A small corpus can show where intuitive labels fail

The project produces a 502-pair, bilingual, searchable corpus with four documented translation profiles, linked visualizations and a 50-entry check sample. It also shows why a classification needs an explicit decision rule: subtitle lines often mix compaction, added context and a change of speech act.

07 · 結論

小型語料也能顯示直覺標籤的限制

本專題建立可搜尋的五百零二組雙語語料，包含四種有紀錄的翻譯剖面、 互相連動的圖表及五十筆核對樣本。它也顯示分類必須有明確判定規則： 同一組字幕往往同時涉及壓縮、補充情境與言語行為改變。

08 · Reflection

What AI helped with, and what it could not decide

AI was effective for extracting requirements, cleaning the workbook, implementing the interface and producing reproducible counts. It was weaker at deciding whether two short lines were pragmatically equivalent. That judgement depends on context, character voice and the surrounding scene.

If the project continued, I would sample directly from the complete English and Chinese tracks with a fixed seed, record timecodes, and ask a second coder to classify a boundary sample. I would also keep a separate field for omitted context rather than using length as a proxy for meaning.

08 · 反思

AI 能協助什麼，不能替人決定什麼

AI 在擷取要求、清理 Excel、建立互動介面及產生可重現統計方面十分有效， 但較難判斷兩句短對白在語用上是否完全等值。這種判斷依賴情境、角色語氣及前後畫面。

若有更多時間，我會直接從完整英粵字幕軌以固定種子抽樣、保留時間碼， 並請第二位編碼者處理邊界樣本。我也會另設「省略情境」欄位， 避免把句長直接當成意義增減的替代指標。
